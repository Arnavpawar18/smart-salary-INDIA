# Developer Rules & Operating Constraints

1. **Strict Surgical Changes Only**:
   - Whenever a change or bug fix is requested, modify **ONLY** the specific target component/lines directly relevant to that request.
   - Do **NOT** touch, refactor, reorganize, or rewrite unrelated files, statutory formulas, tax slabs, database models, or modules unless explicitly instructed by the user.

2. **Preserve Verified Systems**:
   - Maintain the integrity of all verified engines (Income tax calculations, EPF/EPS, ESI, Professional Tax schedules, and DB schemas).
