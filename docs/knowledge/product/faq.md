# SmartSalary FAQ & Help Guide

### 1. What makes SmartSalary different from other tax calculators?
Unlike generic calculators that hardcode simplified approximations, SmartSalary uses a verified statutory evidence repository containing official CBDT ITR JSON schemas, Gazette notifications, and the Income-tax Act, 2025. Every deduction is explainable via an interactive `[Why?]` drawer with deep links to government sources.

### 2. Which Financial Year is currently active?
As of 18 August 2026, the active framework is **FY 2026-27 / Tax Year 2026-27** governed by the **Income-tax Act, 2025** (Assessment Year terminology is abolished under the new Act). Historical calculations for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) remain fully supported under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

### 3. Does my occupation force me into a specific tax regime?
**No.** In SmartSalary, your occupation only customizes the questions asked and the income deductions available to you. The statutory rule engine determines tax liability and regime options objectively.

### 4. How is my privacy protected during report verification?
When someone scans your report's QR code or visits `/verify/<public-report-id>`, they only see confirmation of the report's authenticity, financial year, and creation timestamp. **Zero private financial data** (salary, tax liability, PAN, email) is ever exposed publicly.
