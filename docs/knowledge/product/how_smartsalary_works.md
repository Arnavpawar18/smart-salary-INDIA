# How SmartSalary Works

SmartSalary India is an evidence-grounded salary calculation, tax forecasting, statutory payroll, and financial intelligence platform designed specifically for the Indian workforce.

## Core Philosophy: The Sovereign Invariant
1. **Deterministic Calculations:** Every single rupee shown to you is calculated using verified statutory formulas, not AI approximations.
2. **Authoritative Evidence Lineage:** Every deduction (TDS, PF, ESI, PT) traces directly back to Gazette notifications, CBDT validation schemas, or official Act sections.
3. **No AI Inventions:** Artificial Intelligence is used exclusively for natural-language explanations, document retrieval, and contextual guidance. AI will never silently calculate or alter financial amounts.

## Supported Modes
* **Mode A: Tax Only:** Fast, streamlined income tax liability computation under both the New and Old Tax Regimes for FY 2026-27 (Income-tax Act, 2025) and legacy FY 2025-26.
* **Mode B: Tax + Savings:** Comprehensive cash-flow analysis incorporating income sources, 8 categories of essential and discretionary expenses, and projected savings surplus/deficit.

## Supported Calculation Periods
1. **Monthly:** Standard per-month cash take-home pay.
2. **Quarterly:** 3-month statutory withholding and cash-flow overview.
3. **Six Months (H1/H2):** Half-yearly earnings and tax projections.
4. **Annual:** Full financial year summary ($\sum \text{April}\dots\text{March}$) with per-month seasonal variation support.
