# Statutory Provident Fund (EPFO) Rule Matrix

This document records the **verified statutory contribution parameters** under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

---

## 1. EPFO Contribution Breakdown

| Component | Contributor | Statutory Rate | Statutory Wage Base Cap | Applicability / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Employee EPF** | Employee | **12.00%** | ₹15,000 / month (or actual Basic+DA if opt-in) | Reduces employee take-home |
| **Employer EPF** | Employer | **3.67%** | Equal to (Total 12% employer contribution - 8.33% EPS) | Excluded from employee take-home reduction |
| **Employer EPS** | Employer | **8.33%** | Capped strictly at ₹15,000 / month (Max ₹1,250 / month) | Pension fund allocation |
| **Employer EDLI** | Employer | **0.50%** | Capped strictly at ₹15,000 / month (Max ₹75 / month) | Deposit Linked Insurance |
| **Admin Charges** | Employer | **0.50%** | Statutory wage base | Minimum ₹500/month for active establishment |

---

## 2. Statutory Wage Ceiling & Opt-In Rules

- **Statutory Wage Ceiling**: **₹15,000 per month** (₹1,80,000 per year).
- **Mandatory Coverage**: Employees with Basic + DA $\le$ ₹15,000 per month.
- **Voluntary Higher Wage Contribution**:
  - If employer and employee opt to contribute on actual full basic salary exceeding ₹15,000/month:
    - Employee EPF: 12% on full (Basic + DA).
    - Employer EPS: Capped at 8.33% of ₹15,000 (₹1,250/month).
    - Employer EPF: Balance of 12% on full (Basic + DA) minus ₹1,250.
- **Critical SmartSalary Rule**: Employer contributions (`Employer EPF`, `Employer EPS`, `EDLI`) must NEVER reduce the employee's estimated monthly or annual take-home salary.
