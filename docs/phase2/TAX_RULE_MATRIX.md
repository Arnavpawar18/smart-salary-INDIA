# Statutory Income Tax Rule Matrix (CBDT / ITD)

This document records the **verified statutory tax rules** across FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26, and FY 2026-27 for salaried individuals under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

---

## 1. FY 2025-26 / FY 2026-27 (AY 2026-27 / AY 2027-28) — New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)

### 1.1 Tax Slabs
| Slab Order | Income Range (₹) | Statutory Tax Rate | Marginal Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | ₹0 to ₹4,00,000 | **0.00%** | Nil bracket |
| **2** | ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 | **5.00%** | ₹20,000 on ₹8,00,000 |
| **3** | ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | **10.00%** | ₹40,000 on this slab (Cum: ₹60,000) |
| **4** | ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 | **15.00%** | ₹60,000 on this slab (Cum: ₹1,20,000) |
| **5** | ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 | **20.00%** | ₹80,000 on this slab (Cum: ₹2,00,000) |
| **6** | ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 | **25.00%** | ₹1,00,000 on this slab (Cum: ₹3,00,000) |
| **7** | Above ₹24,00,000 | **30.00%** | Balance income |

### 1.2 Standard Deduction (Section 16(ia))
- **Statutory Amount**: **₹75,000** for salaried individuals under the New Regime.

### 1.3 Section 87A Tax Rebate & Marginal Relief
- **Taxable Income Threshold**: **₹12,00,000**
- **Maximum Rebate Amount**: **₹60,000** (Full rebate for resident individuals with taxable income $\le$ ₹12,00,000).
- **Marginal Relief**: For taxable income slightly above ₹12,00,000, total tax payable shall not exceed the amount by which taxable income exceeds ₹12,00,000.

### 1.4 Surcharges (New Regime)
- ₹50 Lakh to ₹1 Crore: **10%** (with Surcharge Marginal Relief)
- ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore: **15%** (with Surcharge Marginal Relief)
- Above ₹2 Crore: **25%** (capped at 25% under New Regime)

### 1.5 Health & Education Cess
- **Statutory Rate**: **4.00%** on total income tax plus surcharge minus relief.

## 2. FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) — New Tax Regime (Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024)

### 2.1 Tax Slabs
| Slab Order | Income Range (₹) | Statutory Tax Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ₹0 to ₹3,00,000 | **0.00%** |
| **2** | ₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000 | **5.00%** |
| **3** | ₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | **10.00%** |
| **4** | ₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | **15.00%** |
| **5** | ₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000 | **20.00%** |
| **6** | Above ₹15,00,000 | **30.00%** |

### 2.2 Standard Deduction & Section 87A Rebate
- **Standard Deduction**: **₹75,000** (enhanced from ₹50,000 by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024).
- **Section 87A Rebate**: Up to **₹25,000** for taxable income up to **₹7,00,000**.

## 3. Old Tax Regime (All Financial Years)

### 3.1 Tax Slabs (Normal Individual < 60 years)
| Slab Order | Income Range (₹) | Statutory Tax Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ₹0 to ₹2,50,000 | **0.00%** |
| **2** | ₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000 | **5.00%** |
| **3** | ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | **20.00%** |
| **4** | Above ₹10,00,000 | **30.00%** |

### 3.2 Deductions & Rebates
- **Standard Deduction (Section 16(ia))**: **₹50,000**
- **Section 80C**: Up to **₹1,50,000**
- **Section 80D**: Up to **₹25,000** (Self/Family) + ₹25,000/₹50,000 (Parents)
- **Section 87A Rebate**: Up to **₹12,500** for taxable income up to **₹5,00,000**.
- **Cess**: **4.00%**
