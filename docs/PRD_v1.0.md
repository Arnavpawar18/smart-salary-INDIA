# Project PRD: SmartSalary India (v1.0)

## 1. Executive Summary
SmartSalary India is a premium, AI-native fintech platform designed to simplify the complex landscape of Indian payroll, tax compliance, and personal financial optimization. The product serves two primary audiences: **Organizations** (HR/Admin) and **Individual Employees**.

The core value proposition is "Precision Financial Intelligence," leveraging AI to optimize tax regimes (Old vs. New), automate compliance (Form 16/24Q), and provide a cinematic, high-performance user experience.

---

## 2. Product Vision & Goals
- **Mission**: To resolve the friction of Indian payroll and tax planning into a seamless, high-fidelity digital experience.
- **Strategic Objectives**:
  - Achieve 100% compliance accuracy with RBI and Indian DPDP Act standards.
  - Reduce tax optimization time for employees by 80% via AI simulation.
  - Streamline organization-wide payroll reconciliation and disbursement.
  - Establish a "Premium Fintech" aesthetic: Midnight indigo, high-contrast, and performant.

---

## 3. User Personas

### 3.1. The Corporate Administrator (HR/Payroll Manager)
- **Pain Points**: Bulk data errors, state-specific professional tax rules, manual verification of employee proofs, and year-end reconciliation stress.
- **Key Needs**: High-density dashboards, bulk upload validation, audit logs, and one-click payroll approval.

### 3.2. The Individual Employee
- **Pain Points**: Confusion between New vs. Old tax regimes, tedious investment declaration flows, and lack of clarity on take-home salary.
- **Key Needs**: AI-driven tax optimization, instant payslip access, and simple proof submission.

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1. Core Administrative Engine (Company Portal)
- **Entity Management**: Support for multi-entity configurations with custom pay cycles.
- **Salary Structure Builder**: Granular control over pay components (Basic, HRA, LTA, Bonus, etc.).
- **Bulk Operations**: CSV/Excel upload with row-level validation and column mapping.
- **Payroll Approval**: Multi-state workflow (Draft -> Pending -> Approved -> Finalized).
- **Statutory Reporting**: Automated generation of Form 16, Form 24Q, and state-specific professional tax filings.

### 4.2. Employee Self-Service (Individual Portal)
- **Financial Nerve Center**: Dashboard showing earnings, tax liability, and investment status.
- **AI Tax Optimizer**: Real-time simulation of tax savings across different investment scenarios.
- **Tax Proof Hub**: Structured upload and status tracking for 80C, 80D, and HRA proofs.
- **Payslip Intelligence**: Interactive analysis of earnings with historical archives.

### 4.3. AI & Intelligence Layer
- **Tax Intelligence Assistant**: Context-aware chat for answering specific tax/payroll queries.
- **AI Risk Engine**: Proactive detection of discrepancies in payroll batches and tax proofs.

---

## 5. Technical & Design Parameters

### 5.1. Visual Identity (Premium Fintech System)
- **Color Palette**: Midnight Indigo (#0b1326), Surface Brights (#31394d), Accent Indigo (#4f46e5).
- **Typography**: Geist (Sans-serif) for high legibility and professional feel.
- **Layout**: Bento-grid style dashboards for modular information density.
- **Texture**: Subtle noise grain (3% opacity) on dark surfaces to prevent flatness.

### 5.2. Motion & Interaction (2026 Standards)
- **Duration Scale**: Micro (100ms), Component (200ms), Page (350ms).
- **Easing**: Custom cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for snappy, professional settling.
- **Transitions**: Staggered card reveals (40ms offset) and left-to-right line chart drawing.

### 5.3. Localization & Compliance (India)
- **Currency**: INR with Lakh/Crore grouping (₹XX,XX,XXX).
- **Date Format**: DD MMM YYYY.
- **Compliance**: Alignment with Indian DPDP Act (Self-service data export/deletion).
- **Security**: Mandatory 2FA for administrative accounts via Authenticator/Email OTP.
